Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Little Outings is an app for parents. It lists outings for babies and toddlers in a growing list of cities and alerts you when registration opens. We built it so that it doesn't need to know who you are.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't collect, store, sell or share personal information about you or your child.

What stays on your phone

To show the right events, the app saves a few things on your device only:

  • Your child's birth month and year
  • Your city, neighborhood and preferred distance
  • Events you save, events you ask to be alerted about, and events you add yourself

This information never leaves your device and we can't see it. You can erase it at any time in the app under Settings, then "Clear saved events and alerts". Deleting the app also erases it.

Alerts

Registration alerts are scheduled by your phone itself. We don't run a notification server, and we don't receive your device's push token or any record of which events you follow.

The event list

The app downloads the current list of cities and events from little-outings.com. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs of requests, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability. Those logs are covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive them and don't use them to identify anyone.

Links to other websites

When you open an organizer's page, a map or a ticket site from the app, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Little Outings is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. It doesn't collect any information from or about children. The birth month you enter stays on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
