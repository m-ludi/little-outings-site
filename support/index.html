Support

Questions, a missing event, or a date that looks wrong? Email support@little-outings.com. We read every message.

How do registration alerts work?

Tap Alert me on any event with a sign-up date. Your phone alerts you the day before, 15 minutes before, and the moment registration opens. Tap the last alert to go straight to the sign-up page. If the organizer hasn't posted a time of day, you get an alert the evening before and on the morning of release day.

I didn't get an alert

  • Check that notifications are on for Little Outings in your phone's Settings app.
  • On iPhone, a Focus mode such as Sleep or Work can hide alerts. Add Little Outings to the allowed apps.
  • On Android, open Little Outings, go to Settings, and allow exact-time alerts. Without that, Android may deliver alerts a few minutes late.
  • Battery savers on some Android phones delay alerts. Exclude Little Outings from battery optimization.

What does "Date not announced" mean?

The organizer runs this program regularly but hasn't posted the next sign-up date yet. Tap Alert me anyway. When the date appears in our weekly update, the app sets your alerts automatically the next time you open it.

My city isn't listed

Pick "Somewhere else" when the app asks where you live. You can still add events you find and get alerts for them. Email support@little-outings.com to ask for your city. New cities are added based on requests.

Where do the events come from?

Every listing is checked against the organizer's own website, and the list is refreshed every week. Details can change at short notice, so confirm on the official page before you go. Little Outings isn't affiliated with any venue.

Suggest an event or report a mistake

Email support@little-outings.com with the event name and a link to its page. To fix a listing, tell us what's wrong and we'll correct it in the next update.

Delete my data

Everything is stored on your phone. In the app, open Settings and tap "Clear saved events and alerts", or delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.
